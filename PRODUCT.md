# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

QED serves mathematical researchers who need to submit, track, compare, and
independently verify complex proofs. It does not target general chat users.

## Product Purpose

Researchers submit a problem, proving guidance, and verification rules. QED
produces traceable literature evidence, a proof plan, sealed candidates,
independent verification reports, and reproducible proof, report, and manifest
exports. Success means a researcher can audit the evidence chain and the
code-computed decision without trusting a model's claim of completion.

## Positioning

QED turns a mathematical problem into a content-addressed, reproducible research
record verified by independent Codex threads.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, restrained, and auditable.

## Anti-references

No named product or site serves as an anti-reference. QED should not resemble a
marketing website or a general-purpose chat interface.

## Design Principles

- Put evidence and provenance next to each research claim.
- Make independent verification visible in the structure of the workflow.
- Preserve sealed history so researchers can compare decisions and reproduce an export.
- Keep the interface focused on research work, with no promotional framing.
